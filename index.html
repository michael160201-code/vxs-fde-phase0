What the free scan is for

A first-pass diagnostic brief for founders, formulators, and consultants preparing a sensitive beverage or supplement system for pilot production. You can submit ranges, categories, and process conditions instead of a complete proprietary formula.

What it is not

It is not a safety review, regulatory review, medical claim assessment, supplier recommendation, or guarantee of scale-up success. It is a structured risk map for your next validation decision.
